## Ingredients
- 4 Boiled Eggs
- 2 tablespoons oil (Preferably coconut oil)
- ½ teaspoon Mustard Seeds
- ½ teaspoon Fennel Seeds
- 2 cups onions sliced thin (about 2 to 3 medium onions)
- 1 teaspoon Ginger, crushed
- 1 teaspoon Garlic, crushed
- 1 sprig Curry Leaves
- ¼ teaspoon Turmeric Powder
- ¾ to 1 teaspoon Red Chili Powder (adjust to taste)
- ¼ to ½ teaspoon Black Pepper Powder
- 1 teaspoon Garam Masala
- 2 teaspoons Coriander Powder
- 1 medium Tomato, finely chopped (about ⅓ cup)
- ¼ cup hot water
- ½ teaspoon salt (more if needed)

## Directions
1. Boil eggs and deshell. Poke them with a fork randomly or make slits on them. Set aside.
2. Pour oil to a heated pan over medium heat. Add mustard seeds and fennel seeds, allow them to crackle.
3. Add sliced onions and salt. Sauté them until lightly golden.
4. Add crushed ginger-garlic and sauté until the onions turn slightly caramelized.
5. Spoon in turmeric powder, chili powder, black pepper, Garam Masala, and coriander powder. Sauté for a few minutes or until the raw smell goes away.
6. Add finely chopped tomato and cook until it becomes mushy. Pour hot water and mix well. Cook until the water evaporates.
7. Transfer the boiled eggs and a few sprigs of curry leaves. Fry them for 10 minutes, until the onions caramelize and the egg roast turns aromatic. Taste test and add more salt if needed.
8. Serve egg roast hot with appam, puttu, chapathi, or naan.

## Notes
- **Variation:** For a creamier curry, you can add coconut milk towards the end instead of adding water, or add poached eggs instead of boiled eggs.
- **Oil:** Use coconut oil if possible to make the curry for an authentic South Indian flavor.
- **Ginger & Garlic:** Avoid ginger-garlic paste for this recipe, as the crushed garlic-ginger mix lends an authentic taste.
- **Spice Adjustments:** You can use Kashmiri chili powder instead of red chili powder for vibrant color with less heat.
- **Tomatoes:** Avoid using large tomatoes, as tomatoes are added to this recipe mainly for a subtle tang.
- **Curry Leaves:** Do not skip using curry leaves, as they play a significant role in providing authentic flavor.
- **Flavor Balance:** If desired, add half a teaspoon of sugar or jaggery at the end to balance the flavors.
