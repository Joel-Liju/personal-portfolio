## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 2 teaspoons hot water
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

## Directions
1. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
2. Cream together the softened butter, white sugar, and packed brown sugar in a large bowl until the mixture is completely smooth.
3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla extract.
4. Dissolve the baking soda in the hot water. Add this to the batter along with the salt.
5. Gently stir in the all-purpose flour, semisweet chocolate chips, and chopped walnuts (if using).
6. Drop the dough by large spoonfuls onto ungreased baking pans.
7. Bake for about 10 minutes in the preheated oven, or until the edges are nicely browned.

## Notes
- **Baking Soda Trick**: Dissolving the baking soda in hot water before adding it ensures even distribution throughout the dense dough, preventing pockets of metallic taste.
- **Texture Control**: For thicker, bakery-style cookies that do not spread into flat puddles, chill the mixed dough in the refrigerator for 30 to 60 minutes before scooping it onto your baking sheets.
