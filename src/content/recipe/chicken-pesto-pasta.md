## Ingredients
- 250 g (8.8 oz) pasta
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 20 g (1 tablespoon) unsalted butter
- 450 g (1 lb) chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
- 2 large garlic cloves
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning mix
- 130 g (4.5 oz) green basil pesto
- 100 g (3.5 oz) sundried tomatoes
- 6-8 fresh basil leaves, roughly shredded
- 30 g (1 ounce) pine nuts, toasted
- Salt and freshly ground pepper to taste

## Directions
1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil, salt it generously, and cook the pasta al dente according to the directions on the package. Reserve 1-2 cups of pasta water before draining.
2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large, deep pan or Dutch oven and melt the butter in it. Add the chicken and cook for 5-6 minutes on medium heat, or until the chicken is cooked to your liking.
3. Stir in the garlic and Italian seasoning mix and continue to cook for another minute. Next, add the cooked pasta to the pan together with half a cup of pasta water and stir to combine.
4. Stir in the pesto and add a bit more pasta water if it looks too dry. Next, stir in the sundried tomatoes, fresh basil leaves and pine nuts.
5. Season to taste and serve, sprinkled with some extra toasted pine nuts, if you like.

## Notes
- Leftovers will keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days in an airtight container. Reheat in the microwave or serve at room temperature.
- Freezing is not recommended as the texture of pesto pasta will change when thawed.
- Option to add Italian sausage or pancetta for extra flavor.
- Sprinkle freshly grated parmesan cheese or add fresh mozzarella balls on each bowl right before serving.
