## Ingredients
**Chicken:**
- 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour, or as needed
- ½ cup olive oil for frying, or as needed

**Topping:**
- ½ cup prepared tomato sauce
- ¼ cup fresh mozzarella, cubed
- ¼ cup chopped fresh basil
- ½ cup grated provolone cheese
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

## Directions
**Prep and Fry:**
1. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C).
2. Place chicken breasts between two sheets of plastic wrap and pound to 1/2-inch thickness. Season with salt and black pepper.
3. Whisk eggs in a shallow bowl. Combine panko breadcrumbs and 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese in a second bowl. Place flour in a third bowl.
4. Dredge chicken breasts in flour, shake off excess, dip in egg, and coat thoroughly with the panko mixture.
5. Heat 1/2 cup olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry chicken until golden brown, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Transfer chicken to a baking dish.

**Bake:**
1. Top each chicken breast with 2 tablespoons tomato sauce, mozzarella, basil, provolone, and remaining 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon olive oil.
2. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted and golden, 10 to 15 minutes.
