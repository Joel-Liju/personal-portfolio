## Ingredients
- 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cubed
- 3/4 teaspoon garlic salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup dry white rice
- 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream, room temperature
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese

## Directions
1. Season the cubed chicken breasts with the garlic salt and ground black pepper.
2. Spray a large skillet with oil and cook the seasoned chicken over medium-high heat for 5-7 minutes until browned. 
3. Add the minced garlic to the skillet and sauté for 1 minute more.
4. Add the dry white rice and chicken broth to the pan with the chicken. Bring the liquid to a boiling point.
5. Cover the skillet tightly with a well-fitting lid and reduce the heat slightly. Cook for 12-15 minutes until most of the liquid is absorbed. Try not to remove the lid during this time so the moisture stays trapped inside.
6. Once the liquid is absorbed, remove the lid and add in the heavy cream and fresh spinach. Stir everything together.
7. Replace the cover and cook for another 5-7 minutes until the spinach is wilted and the cream is mostly absorbed.
8. Remove from the heat and stir in the freshly grated Parmesan cheese right at the end before serving.

## Notes
- **Room Temperature Cream**: Always bring the heavy cream to room temperature before adding it to the skillet. If it is too cold straight out of the refrigerator, the sudden temperature change can cause it to curdle.
- **Keep the Lid Sealed**: Try your best not to remove the lid while the rice is simmering. Every time you peek, critical steam and moisture escape, which can lead to hard, chewy, undercooked rice grains.
- **Pan Selection**: Use a large, high-sided non-stick skillet or a Dutch oven with a well-fitted lid to ensure even cooking and prevent the rice from sticking to the bottom.
