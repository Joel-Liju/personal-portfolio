## Ingredients
- 1 pound shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 1 ½ teaspoons onion powder
- 1 ½ teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon seasoning salt
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper (add more if you like more heat)
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- ½ teaspoon thyme
- ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)

## Directions
1. Combine all spices in a bowl and toss with shrimp to coat evenly.
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
3. Add shrimp and cook 2–3 minutes per side or until the shrimp are opaque and pink. Do not overcook.

I like how a few pantry spices turn shrimp into a bold, savory centerpiece; a little extra cayenne makes it easy to dial up the heat.

## Notes
- **Extra Crispy Coating**: For extra crispy shrimp, add ½ teaspoon of baking powder to the seasoning mix.
- **Thawing Tip**: If using frozen shrimp, defrost them in the refrigerator. For a quick thaw, place shrimp in a colander and run cool (not warm) water over them until thawed. Ensure the shrimp are patted dry before adding the seasoning so the spices adhere properly.
- **Leftovers**: Store in a covered container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat wrapped loosely in foil in an oven at 300°F (150°C) for about 15 minutes, or steam in the microwave with a little water or broth in 30-second intervals to avoid overcooking.
