## Ingredients
**For the Garlic Mayo**
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 1 clove garlic, minced

**For the Sandwiches**
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 lb beef round steak, cut into thin strips
- 2 green bell peppers, cut into ¼-inch strips
- 2 onions, sliced into rings
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 4 hoagie rolls, split lengthwise and toasted
- 1 (8 oz) package shredded mozzarella cheese
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano

## Directions
1. Preheat oven to 500°F (260°C).
2. Mix mayonnaise and minced garlic together in a small bowl; set aside.
3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Add beef, green bell peppers, and onions to the skillet. Season with salt and black pepper. Sauté until beef is browned and vegetables are tender, about 10 to 15 minutes.
5. Spread garlic mayonnaise inside each toasted hoagie roll.
6. Divide beef and vegetable mixture evenly among the hoagie rolls.
7. Top sandwiches with shredded mozzarella cheese and sprinkle with dried oregano.
8. Place sandwiches on a baking sheet and bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted and bubbly, about 3 to 5 minutes.

## What I liked

I like the combination of tender steak and sweet, softened peppers, especially with garlicky mayo adding a creamy, savory note to the sandwich.

## Notes
- Round steak works well when thinly sliced across the grain; ribeye or sirloin can also be substituted for extra tenderness.
- For a classic variation, try adding sliced mushrooms or swapping mozzarella for provolone or Cheez Whiz.
