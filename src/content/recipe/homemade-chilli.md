## Ingredients
**Chili Base**
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 (15 ounce) can tomato sauce
- 1 (15 ounce) can kidney beans
- 1 (14.5 ounce) can stewed tomatoes

**Seasonings & Liquids**
- 1 1/2 cups water (or as needed for desired consistency)
- 1 teaspoon chili powder (or more to taste)
- 1 pinch garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Directions
**Preparation & Browning**
1. Place the ground beef and chopped onion in a large saucepan over medium heat. 
2. Cook and stir until the meat is thoroughly browned and the onion is tender, about 5 to 7 minutes.

**Simmering**
1. Stir in the tomato sauce, kidney beans, and stewed tomatoes with their juice into the saucepan.
2. Add water if you prefer a thinner consistency. 
3. Season the mixture with chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. 
4. Bring the chili to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover the pan and let it simmer for 15 minutes before serving.

## Notes
- Consistency: The water in this recipe is completely optional. If you prefer a thick, hearty chili, omit the water and let the juices from the stewed tomatoes and sauce form the base.
- Customization: To easily turn this into a vegetarian chili, omit the ground beef entirely and add a second can of kidney beans, black beans, or pinto beans. 
- Flavor Boost: If you like a deeper flavor profile, consider increasing the chili powder to 1 tablespoon or adding a pinch of ground cumin.
