## Ingredients
**Chicken & Seasoning**
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

**Garlic Parmesan Rice & Spinach**
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup long-grain white rice
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 cups fresh spinach, roughly chopped
- 2 tablespoons butter

**Garnish**
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Directions
**Preparation & Chicken**
1. Season both sides of the chicken breasts with garlic powder, paprika, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper. Set aside for a few minutes to let the seasoning settle.
2. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned chicken breasts and sear them for 5 to 6 minutes per side, or until they are golden brown and cooked through. Transfer the chicken to a plate and tent loosely with foil to keep warm.

**Rice & Spinach Base**
3. Reduce the skillet heat to medium and add the butter. Once melted, stir in the minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant, being careful not to let it burn.
4. Pour in the uncooked long-grain white rice and stir to coat it evenly in the garlic butter. 
5. Add the chicken broth, bring it to a gentle simmer, then cover and cook for 15 minutes. Stir occasionally to ensure the rice doesn't stick to the bottom.
6. Once the rice is almost tender and most of the broth is absorbed, stir in the heavy cream and grated Parmesan cheese. Mix well until the sauce becomes creamy.
7. Add the chopped spinach and cook for another 2-3 minutes, just until the spinach is wilted and blended into the rice.
8. Return the chicken breasts to the skillet, spoon some of the creamy rice over the top, and cook everything together for 2-3 more minutes until heated completely through. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley before serving.

## Notes
- **Cheese Selection**: Use freshly grated Parmesan cheese rather than pre-shredded varieties packaged in a tub. Fresh cheese melts beautifully into the rice without creating a grainy texture.
- **Spinach Timing**: Add the spinach at the very end as directed to prevent it from overcooking, which makes it mushy and dulls its vibrant green color.
- **Leftovers & Reheating**: Leftovers store beautifully in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. When reheating, splash a small amount of extra chicken broth, milk, or water into the rice to restore its luxurious, creamy consistency.
- **Skillet Space**: Avoid overcrowding the pan when searing the chicken breasts; giving them adequate space ensures they get a proper golden brown crust rather than steaming.
