## Ingredients
**Pork Base**
- 1 pound thin boneless pork chops, cut into 1/4-inch strips
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil

**Honey Garlic Sauce**
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 tablespoon chopped garlic
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper

## Directions
**Preparation & Cooking**
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, honey, garlic, sesame oil, and crushed red pepper until well combined.
2. Heat the olive oil over high heat in a large skillet. Add the thinly sliced pork to the pan and quickly sauté on each side for 2 minutes. Remove the pork from the pan and set aside.
3. Pour the sauce mixture into the same skillet and reduce the heat to medium-low. Allow the sauce to simmer and thicken for about 5 minutes.
4. Add the pork back into the skillet and toss thoroughly to coat. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes, or until the pork is fully cooked through and the sauce becomes a thick, sticky glaze.
5. Serve hot over a bed of rice or noodles, and garnish with fresh cilantro, green onions, or sesame seeds if desired.

## What I liked

I like the sweet-salty garlic glaze and quick-seared pork; red pepper keeps the honey from tasting too sweet and makes it great over rice.

## Notes
- **Pork Cut**: Thin-cut boneless pork loin chops work best because they cut into strips easily. The quick sear over high heat keeps the lean meat tender without drying it out.
- **Optional Thickener**: If you prefer an extra-thick, glossy takeout-style glaze, you can whisk 1 tablespoon of cornstarch directly into the cold sauce ingredients before adding it to the skillet.
- **Heat Adjustment**: Adjust the crushed red pepper to 1/4 teaspoon if you prefer a milder flavor, or swap it for a splash of sriracha for a different type of kick.
- **Serving Suggestions**: This dish pairs wonderfully with steamed jasmine rice, udon noodles, or stir-fried greens like broccoli and bok choy. Leftovers make an excellent cold topping for salad or a rice bowl the next day.
