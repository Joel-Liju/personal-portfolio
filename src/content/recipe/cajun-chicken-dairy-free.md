## Ingredients
**Cajun Seasoning**
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- *Note: Alternatively, use 1 tablespoon of your favorite pre-made Cajun spice mix.*

**Chicken Pasta**
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 14 ounces pre-cooked smoked sausage, sliced (like Andouille)
- 2 Tablespoons olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 12 ounces penne pasta (gluten-free if needed)
- 1 (14.5 ounce) can fire roasted tomatoes (with juice)
- 2 cups chicken broth or bone broth
- 1/2 cup unsweetened full-fat coconut cream (or heavy cream if not dairy-free)
- Chopped parsley or green onion for topping

## Directions
**Preparation**
1. Combine all the Cajun seasoning ingredients in a medium bowl.
2. Cut the chicken into 1/2-inch cubes, add to the bowl with the seasoning, and toss to coat evenly.

**Cooking**
1. Heat olive oil in a deep skillet over medium-high heat until hot and rippling. Add the seasoned chicken cubes to the hot skillet and cook until browned on all sides, about 3-5 minutes.
2. Add the diced yellow onion and sliced smoked sausage to the pan with the chicken. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until the onion is soft and translucent, about 2-3 minutes.
3. Add the uncooked penne pasta, the entire can of fire-roasted tomatoes with their juice, and the chicken broth. Stir until everything is well combined.
4. Close the lid of the pan and allow it to come to a boil over medium-high heat.
5. Once boiling, turn the heat down to medium-low and simmer covered for about 10 minutes, stirring every few minutes to prevent sticking. When done, the mixture should be thick and saucy, and the pasta should be tender.
6. Remove the lid, add the coconut cream (or heavy cream) to the pan, and mix until completely melted, combined, and creamy.
7. Serve hot, garnished with fresh chopped parsley or green onions.

## Notes
- **Pasta Tip:** If you are using gluten-free pasta, pull the dish off the heat slightly before the pasta reaches perfect al dente, as it will continue to cook and soften in the hot residual sauce.
- **Coconut Flavor:** Don't worry about a heavy coconut flavor if using coconut cream; the bold Cajun spices completely mask it, leaving behind only a silky, rich dairy-free texture.
- **Heat Control:** Andouille sausage and cayenne pepper bring a prominent kick. For a milder version, swap the Andouille for kielbasa or smoked chicken sausage, and cut back on the cayenne pepper.
- **Sauce Management:** Make sure to stir occasionally while it simmers so the pasta doesn't stick to the bottom of the pan and the liquid reduces evenly.
