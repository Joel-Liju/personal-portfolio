## Ingredients
**Egg sandwich filling**
- 6 soft boiled eggs, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon whole egg mayonnaise
- 2 teaspoons dijon mustard
- 2 teaspoons finely chopped chives (or green onion)
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/8 teaspoon cooking/kosher salt

**Sandwich**
- 8 slices soft white sandwich bread
- Soft salted butter, for spreading on bread

## Directions
1. **Mash egg**: Place the eggs in a bowl and crush with a fork. Once mostly broken up, use a potato masher to mash them up really well. Smaller egg white bits equal a creamier filling.
2. **Filling**: Add remaining filling ingredients (mayonnaise, dijon mustard, chives, black pepper, and salt) and gently stir to combine.
3. **Make sandwich**: Butter the bread slices evenly. Divide the egg filling between 4 pieces of bread, spreading evenly from edge to edge. Top with the remaining bread slices. Trim the crusts off if desired, then cut into your preferred shape (rectangles, squares, or diagonals).
4. **Serve**: Always serve at room temperature for the best flavor profile.

## What I liked

I like the combination of rich egg yolks and sharp Dijon, with chives lifting the filling and black pepper giving every bite a little edge.

## Notes
- **Soft Boiled Egg Trick**: The secret to this creamy filling is soft boiling rather than hard boiling the eggs. To achieve the perfect texture, boil 4 inches of water, gently lower the eggs in, and boil for exactly 8 minutes. Immediately cool them in cold water for 10 minutes before peeling. This leaves the yolks creamy so they emulsify with minimal extra mayo.
- **Bread Choice**: Use thick-sliced soft white sandwich bread. Avoid chewy, crusty artisan style breads (like sourdough) because the creamy egg filling will ooze completely out of the sides when you bite down.
- **Pro Cutting Tip**: If you want perfectly neat edges for high tea or fancy presentation, wrap the assembled sandwiches and refrigerate them for 1 hour to let the filling firm up before slicing off the crusts. Just be sure to bring them back up to room temperature before serving so the flavors shine through.
