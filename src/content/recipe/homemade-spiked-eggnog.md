## Ingredients
**Eggnog Base**
- 6 eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 cups half-and-half
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla

**The Spike**
- 1 cup liquor of choice (rum, bourbon, whiskey, or brandy)

**Serving**
- Grated nutmeg or cinnamon

## Directions
**Preparation**
1. In a large mixing bowl, beat the eggs with an electric mixer until they are creamy and lightened in color.
2. Add the sugar and stir with a spoon until all the sugar is dissolved into the egg mixture.
3. Add the half-and-half, heavy cream, and vanilla. Stir to combine.
4. Strain the mixture into an airtight container.
5. Add the liquor and stir to combine. Store in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
6. When ready to serve, top each glass of eggnog with a sprinkle of freshly grated nutmeg or cinnamon.

## Notes
- **Raw Egg Safety**: This recipe uses raw eggs. If you are concerned about consuming raw eggs, use store-bought pasteurized eggs. Alternatively, you can heat the egg mixture in a double boiler to 140°F (60°C) before adding the alcohol to ensure safety.
- **Straining**: While optional, straining the mixture is recommended to remove any unmixed egg white, which ensures a smoother, more luxurious texture in the finished drink.
- **Alcohol Customization**: Dark spiced rum is a popular choice for its nuanced flavor, but you can use bourbon, brandy, or whiskey depending on your preference. If serving non-drinkers, simply omit the liquor.
- **Making Ahead**: This eggnog can be made a couple of days in advance and stored in the refrigerator; some enthusiasts even allow it to "age" for a few weeks for a deeper flavor.
