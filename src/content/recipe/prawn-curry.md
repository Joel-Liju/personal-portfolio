## Ingredients
**Sauteing prawns:**
- 250 gms prawns - clean and devein the prawns well
- 1 tablespoon oil
- salt - to taste
- ½ teaspoon turmeric powder

**Cooking the prawn masala curry:**
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 2 slit green chillies
- 2 medium size onions finely sliced
- salt to taste
- ½ teaspoon ginger garlic paste
- 2 tomatoes finely sliced
- 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
- ½ teaspoon roasted coriander seeds powder
- 2 teaspoon coconut powder (desiccated coconut or fresh coconut)
- 1 cup water
- 6-7 curry leaves
- 1 tablespoon chopped coriander leaves

## Directions
**Sauteing the prawns:**
1. In a wok, add oil and heat it.
2. Add prawns, add salt and turmeric powder, mix well.
3. Saute the prawns until the water gets absorbed and cook for 5-10 minutes.
4. Switch off the flame and rest the prawns aside for later use.

**Cooking the prawns:**
1. In a separate wok, add oil and heat it.
2. Add green chillies, add sliced onions and cook the onions until they turn soft.
3. Add salt as per taste, ginger garlic paste and saute until raw smell of ginger garlic paste disappears.
4. Add chopped tomatoes, mix well.
5. Add red chilli powder, roasted coriander seeds powder, mix well and cook the masala until oil leaves the sides.
6. Add coconut powder, mix well and cook the gravy for few minutes.
7. Add the sauteed prawns into the masala gravy, mix carefully and cook for 4-5 minutes.
8. Add 1 cup water and stir well.
9. Add curry leaves, coriander leaves, mix well and cook the gravy on low flame for few minutes until oil appears at the sides.
10. Switch off the flame.
11. Serve the prawn curry masala with steamed rice.

## What I liked

I like the fragrant curry leaves and warm spice in the tomato-onion masala, with coconut rounding out the sauce around the prawns.

## Notes
- Coconut milk can be added instead of coconut powder if available.
- Cleaning of prawns properly is a very important step, mainly deveining the prawns.
- Do not overcook the prawns as they tend to get harder if cooked for a longer time.
