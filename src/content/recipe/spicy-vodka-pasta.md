## Ingredients
**Spicy Vodka Sauce & Pasta**
- ¼ cup (60mL) extra virgin olive oil
- 4 oz (114g) guanciale, cut into ⅛ inch (3mm) cubes
- 2 red Fresno chilies, finely chopped
- 5 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 2.5 tsp (13g) granulated sugar
- 3 Tbsp (48g) gochujang
- 1 cup (256g) tomato paste
- 1 Tbsp (15mL) vodka
- ¼ cup (60mL) water
- 1.5 cups (360mL) heavy cream
- 1 lb (454g) dried rigatoni (or homemade)
- ¾ cup (75g) grated pecorino cheese
- 2 Tbsp (28g) unsalted butter, cold and cubed
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Freshly grated Parmigiano Reggiano, to taste
- Freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

**Optional Homemade Pasta**
- 500g semolina flour, plus more for dusting
- 250g water, at room temperature, plus more as needed

## Directions
**Spicy Vodka Sauce & Pasta**
1. Fill a 6-quart pot about halfway with water and bring to a boil over high heat. Season generously with salt until it is almost as salty as the ocean.
2. In a 5-quart saute pan, add your olive oil and guanciale. Set the heat to medium-low and stir often until the guanciale is crispy and browned, about 4-5 minutes.
3. Increase the heat to medium, then add the Fresno chilies and garlic. Season with a pinch of salt and saute until fragrant and softened, about 1 minute.
4. Stir in the sugar, then add the gochujang and tomato paste. Cook, stirring often, for 2-3 minutes, or until the mixture begins to thicken, caramelize, and stick to the bottom of the pan.
5. At this point, add your pasta to the boiling water and cook according to package directions if using store-bought, until al dente. (If using fresh homemade rigatoni, cook for 2-3 minutes or until al dente).
6. Deglaze the sauce with the vodka and water, stirring with a wooden or heat-proof spoon or spatula to scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
7. Add the heavy cream, bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then immediately reduce the heat to low and simmer for 1-2 minutes. Once the sauce is done, taste for salt and add more if needed.
8. Add your cooked pasta directly into the sauce pot and toss to combine. Turn off the heat and vigorously stir in your grated pecorino and cold cubed butter.
9. Add additional pasta water to aid in the emulsification and coating as needed, about 1 tablespoon at a time (avoid adding too much to prevent a watery sauce). Taste and adjust salt levels one final time.
10. Serve in a bowl or on a plate topped with freshly grated Parmigiano Reggiano (or more pecorino) and finished with freshly cracked black pepper.

**Optional Homemade Pasta**
1. Combine the semolina flour and water in a large mixing bowl. Stir together with your hands just until it comes together, then knead until it forms a cohesive ball. Add a tablespoon or so of water if necessary to help it bind.
2. Set up a baking sheet lined with a piece of semolina-dusted parchment paper, and attach a rigatoni-shaped pasta extruder to a stand mixer.
3. Turn the mixer to speed 6 and slowly feed small balls of the pasta dough into the machine. 
4. Press through the machine, cut to your desired length (about 2 inches or 5cm), and repeat with the rest of the dough.
5. Gently toss the extruded pasta with more semolina flour, then let it dry uncovered overnight in the refrigerator.

## Notes
- **The Secret Ingredient**: The key twist to this specific vodka pasta recipe is the addition of gochujang (Korean chili paste), which injects a unique layer of sweet, salty, and savory depth alongside the traditional tomato paste.
- **Emulsification Process**: Adding the cheese and butter *off the heat* is crucial for creating a velvety, glossy sauce emulsion. The cold butter melts slowly into the residual heat, preventing the sauce from splitting.
- **Egg-Free Pasta**: The optional homemade rigatoni uses a classic southern Italian style dough consisting purely of semolina flour and water, making it sturdier and perfectly optimized for pushing through a mechanical extruder.
