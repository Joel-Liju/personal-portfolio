## Ingredients
**Basic Oatmeal**
- 1 cup rolled oats (old-fashioned oats)
- 2 cups water (or a mix of water and milk)
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- Optional: A splash of milk or cream to serve

**Common Mix-ins & Toppings**
- Fruit (fresh berries, sliced banana, or dried fruit)
- Nuts and seeds (walnuts, pecans, almonds, chia seeds)
- Sweeteners (maple syrup, honey, or brown sugar)
- Spices (cinnamon, nutmeg, or ginger)

## Directions
**Cooking the Oats**
1. Combine the oats, water (or liquid), and salt in a small saucepan.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat.
3. Once boiling, reduce the heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally.
4. Cook for about 5 to 7 minutes, or until the oats are tender and have absorbed most of the liquid. The cooking time may vary slightly depending on how thick you prefer your oatmeal.
5. Remove from heat and let sit for a minute to thicken.

**Serving**
1. Transfer the oatmeal to bowls.
2. Add your choice of toppings and mix-ins.

## Notes
- Texture: This recipe uses rolled oats for a chewy texture. If you use steel-cut oats, the cooking time will increase significantly (usually 20–30 minutes).
- Liquid Ratio: The standard ratio is 1 part oats to 2 parts liquid. Use milk instead of water for a creamier consistency.
- Make Ahead: You can prepare a larger batch and store it in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; add a splash of water or milk when reheating.
