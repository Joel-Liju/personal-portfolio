## Ingredients
**Dip Base**
- 1 (10 ounce) package frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
- 1 (16 ounce) container sour cream
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1 (1.8 ounce) package dry leek soup mix (or Knorr vegetable recipe mix)

**Optional Additions**
- 1 (8 ounce) can water chestnuts, drained and chopped
- 3 green onions, chopped
- 1 large round loaf sourdough bread (for serving bowl)

## Directions
**Preparation**
1. Ensure the thawed frozen spinach is thoroughly squeezed dry using paper towels or a clean kitchen towel to remove all excess moisture.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the sour cream, mayonnaise, and dry leek or vegetable soup mix. Stir until completely smooth and the seasoning is evenly distributed.
3. Fold in the squeezed spinach, along with the chopped water chestnuts and green onions if using. 

**Chilling & Serving**
1. Cover the mixing bowl and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (ideally 6 hours or overnight) to allow the dry soup flavors to fully hydrate and meld with the dairy base.
2. If serving in a bread bowl, slice the top off the sourdough loaf and hollow out the center, leaving a 1/2-inch shell. Tear the removed bread into bite-sized chunks.
3. Spoon the chilled spinach dip into the hollowed-out loaf just before serving. Arrange the reserved bread chunks, crackers, or fresh vegetable crudités around the bowl for dipping.

## Notes
- **Moisture Control**: Squeezing the spinach completely dry is the absolute most important step of this process. Leaving water behind will dilute the base and result in a watery, separated dip.
- **Soup Mix Variants**: While the traditional recipe specifically calls for dry leek soup mix or Knorr vegetable recipe mix, onion soup mix can be substituted in a pinch, though it will alter the flavor profile significantly.
- **Lighter Alternatives**: To lower the fat content and calories, you can safely substitute the sour cream with nonfat or whole-milk plain Greek yogurt without sacrificing the necessary tangy flavor profile.
