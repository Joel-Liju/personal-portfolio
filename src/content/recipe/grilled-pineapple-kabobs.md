## Ingredients
**For the marinade**
- ½ cup olive oil
- ½ cup chopped cilantro
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 4 teaspoons minced ginger (or ginger paste)
- 2 limes, juiced
- 2 teaspoons salt

**To build the skewers**
- 1 lb chicken (boneless, skinless thighs work best), cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 medium red onion, sliced into 1-inch pieces
- Pineapple, cut into 1-inch cubes

## Directions
**Marination & Prep**
1. Combine the ingredients for the marinade in a large bowl or ziplock bag.
2. Add the chicken to the marinade and stir to coat. Cover or seal the container, and let it marinate for at least an hour, or up to 24 hours. If marinating for longer than an hour, place it back into a refrigerator or cooler.
3. In the meantime, fire up your grill or get your campfire going to medium-high to high heat.

**Assembly & Cooking**
1. Build the kabobs by threading the chicken, onions, and pineapple onto your skewers. Brush with a bit of oil.
2. Grill the kabobs over medium-high to high heat, turning occasionally so they cook evenly, until the chicken is cooked through – about 10 minutes total (internal temperature of 165°F).
3. Remove from the grill and enjoy!

## Notes
- **At-home prep (optional):** Chicken and vegetables can be pre-chopped ahead of time. The marinade ingredients can be combined in a small, sealable jar. If making this on your first evening at camp, the chicken and marinade can be combined for up to 24 hours (longer than that affects the chicken's texture).
- **Pineapple options:** Fresh pineapple can be used—either from a whole pineapple or fresh pineapple spears. Alternatively, you can use canned pineapple chunks (ensure they are drained well before using).
- **Equipment tips:** Flat, metal skewers are ideal because they prevent ingredients from spinning around and are reusable. If using wooden skewers, make sure to soak them in water for at least 30 minutes prior to grilling to prevent burning. Use an instant-read thermometer to accurately ensure the chicken reaches a safe internal temperature of 165°F.
