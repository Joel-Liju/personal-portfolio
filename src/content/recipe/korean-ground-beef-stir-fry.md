## Ingredients
**Stir Fry Base**
- 1 pound lean ground beef (90% lean)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 cups broccoli florets (or other stir-fry vegetables like sliced bell peppers, shredded carrots, or mushrooms)

**Sauce**
- 1/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/4 cup reduced-sodium soy sauce
- 2 teaspoons sesame oil
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch (note: estimated to thicken stir-fry sauce)

**Serving & Garnish**
- Cooked rice or noodles for serving
- Sliced green onions
- Sesame seeds

## Directions
**Preparation & Cooking**
1. In a large skillet or wok, cook the ground beef and minced garlic over medium-high heat, breaking it into crumbles, for 5-7 minutes or until no longer pink. Drain any excess grease if necessary.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the brown sugar, soy sauce, sesame oil, ground ginger, crushed red pepper flakes, black pepper, and cornstarch until smooth.
3. Add the broccoli florets (and any other desired stir-fry vegetables) directly into the skillet with the beef. 
4. Pour the sauce mixture over the beef and vegetables. 
5. Simmer and toss everything together for 3-5 minutes over medium heat until the sauce has thickened, coats the beef beautifully, and the vegetables reach a tender-crisp texture.
6. Serve immediately over warm cooked rice or noodles, and garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- **Dietary Adjustments**: To make this keto or low-carb friendly, swap the brown sugar for a brown-style monk fruit sweetener or erythritol, and serve the stir-fry over cauliflower rice or zucchini noodles. 
- **Protein Alternatives**: This flavor profile works exceptionally well if you choose to swap the ground beef out for ground pork, ground turkey, or even scrambled tofu for a meat-free option.
- **Serving Suggestion**: For an authentic, probiotic-rich crunch, serve this stir-fry with a side of kimchi.
- **Storage & Reheating**: Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days. Reheat in a skillet over medium heat with a splash of water or broth to loosen up the sauce, or microwave in 30-second bursts.
