## Ingredients
**For the Chicken & Marinade**
- 2 lbs chicken thighs, sliced thin
- 3-4 garlic cloves, peeled
- 2 tablespoons hot pepper paste or tomato paste
- 1/4 cup yogurt
- 1 tablespoon white vinegar (or lemon juice)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon allspice
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon cardamom
- 1/2 teaspoon clove
- 1/2 teaspoon MSG
- 1/2 teaspoon ginger (optional)
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- Salt to taste

**For the Cooking Process**
- 3 tablespoons beef tallow or neutral oil

**For the Red Sauce**
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/4 cup water
- Salt to taste

**For Assembly**
- Large pita bread or flatbread
- Toum (garlic sauce), store-bought or homemade
- Thinly sliced pickles (wild cucumber or Arabic pickles)
- Pomegranate molasses

## Directions
**Preparation and Marinade**
1. Slice the chicken thighs into very thin strips (about 1/4-inch thick) and place them into a large mixing bowl.
2. In a food processor, blender, or large bowl, combine the garlic, hot pepper paste (or tomato paste), yogurt, white vinegar (or lemon juice), olive oil, and the shawarma spice blend (cumin, coriander, allspice, smoked paprika, cardamom, clove, MSG, optional ginger/cayenne, and salt). Blend or whisk until smooth.
3. Pour the marinade over the sliced chicken and mix thoroughly, ensuring every piece is completely coated. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for at least a couple of hours, or overnight.

**The Red Sauce**
1. Heat 1/4 cup of oil in a small pan over medium heat. Add the tomato paste or hot pepper paste and let it cook down until slightly toasted and fragrant.
2. Stir in the cayenne pepper, ketchup, and water. Mix until it forms a thick, glossy, uniform paste. Season with salt to taste and set aside.

**Cooking and Smoking**
1. Preheat your oven on high broil. Place a heavy-duty sheet tray inside the oven for 10–15 minutes until it becomes screaming hot.
2. Carefully remove the hot sheet tray and add the beef tallow or neutral oil, spreading it quickly across the surface to melt.
3. Lay the marinated chicken onto the hot tray in a single, thin, even layer. 
4. Slide it back under the broiler and cook for about 10–15 minutes, flipping halfway through, watching closely until the edges char, crisp up, and caramelize. Remove from the oven.
5. Create a small bowl out of aluminum foil and place it in the center of the cooked chicken tray. Pour a small splash of oil into the foil bowl. 
6. Heat a piece of natural lump charcoal until red hot, drop it carefully into the oil bowl to create immediate smoke, and instantly cover the entire tray tightly with foil or a lid. Let the chicken smoke for 4–5 minutes, then discard the charcoal and foil bowl.

**Assembly**
1. Split open a flat pita bread. If desired, lightly dip the pita into the remaining pan drippings on the sheet tray for extra flavor.
2. Spread a generous amount of toum down the middle of the bread.
3. Pile a hefty portion of the smoky, crispy chicken shawarma over the toum, then add a layer of thinly sliced pickles. 
4. Drizzle with a touch of pomegranate molasses, then fold, tuck, and roll the pita up tightly.
5. Brush the outside of the rolled wrap with a bit of the prepared red sauce glaze.
6. Place the wrap seam-side down in a clean pan over medium-low heat and toast until the bread becomes golden brown and crispy. Serve hot with extra toum for dipping.

## What I liked

I like the layers of garlic, smoky spice, tangy pickles, and sweet-tart pomegranate molasses—each bite gives the crisp chicken plenty of contrast.

## Notes
- **Beef Tallow Magic**: Using beef tallow on the preheated sheet pan is the secret weapon for replicating that authentic vertical-spit street food texture. It gives the chicken crispier, deeply savory edges than neutral oil alone.
- **Charcoal "Dum" Smoking**: The charcoal smoking step (called *Fahm* texturally) adds the distinct charred grill flavor that you normally only get from hours on a commercial rotating spit. Ensure your kitchen is well-ventilated during this brief step.
- **Hot Pepper Paste**: If you can find Turkish pepper paste (*Biber Salçası*), it elevates the marinade and red sauce significantly over standard tomato paste by providing a deeper, slightly smoky base flavor.
- **Smoky**: Ensure that, the house doesn't get too smoky.
