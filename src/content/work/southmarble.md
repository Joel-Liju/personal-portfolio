This is aimed to be a website that was created, so that, clients can simply see a brochure, or the catalog of options for marble that is available as a counter tops for kitchens and sinks. In addition to that, a contact us page was created used Whatsapp link, to make the clients have an easy way of connecting with the owner of the company, and indicate what is needed, or if a quick quote is needed for their homes.

This never ended up being in production, as the end users didn't have the resources to see it to finish, but figured I will still showcase it, as it was pretty close to being done.
