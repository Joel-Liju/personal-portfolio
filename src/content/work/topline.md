This is a simple brochure website for the company based out of UAE called Topline. It is primarily going to be used to showcase the work done by the company, and have a point of reference for the different services offered. In addition to that, this website offers a way for the clients to get in contact with the company.

**Result:** A simple website was created with Astro, and using AI, the template that was used was improved. In addition to that, used emailing services to be able to receive emails from clients. 
Finally, integrated whatsapp business into the website, so that clients can access the business information via the whatsapp interface.
