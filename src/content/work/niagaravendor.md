A project created for Niagara College when working with them, to provide a webform which can be used by companies to sign up for a career fair, where the company can hire prospects from the College. This is created so that, it is easy for the company to register for the fair, request food, or any other potential requirements that they have. 

In addition to that, it gives the organizers of this event, the ability to see the statistics for the event and know how much money has been raised, how many companies have registered, how many individuals are coming to the event, what are the food that is required. Essentially giving the client a dashboard to view data regarding the event. 

This required a full rewrite, from dotnet to dotnet, as initially there was quite a lot of javascript, and a lot of requirements changed for this website. Therefore, a full rewrite was done, and added a new dashboard into it as well.

It is now an integral part of the college's fund raising efforts.
