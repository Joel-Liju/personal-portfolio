## What I like about it

It is one of my favorite board games, with a lot of interesting combinations of expansions that keep adding depth of strategy to the overall game. In Dominion, you are a monarch in a small, pleasant kingdom of rivers and evergreens, but you want a bigger dominion. Each player starts with an identical deck of cards and, through the course of the game, acquires more cards to build a more efficient deck, ultimately aiming to accumulate the most Victory Points by the time the game ends.

## Notes

### Beginner

- **The "Big Money" strategy:** If you are unsure what to buy, focus on Treasure cards (Silver and Gold). Having a high buying power is often more reliable than a complicated engine that misfires.
- **Trash junk:** Use cards that allow you to trash (remove from the game) your starting Copper and Estate cards. Thinning your deck is the single most effective way to ensure you draw your powerful cards more often.
- **Focus on synergies:** Try to buy cards that work well together. For example, if you buy a card that grants +Actions, make sure you have other Action cards in your deck to play with those extra actions.

### Advanced

- **Engine building:** Transition from Big Money to engine building by drafting "Village" (or similar +Action/+Card) style cards to create long, multi-card chains. This allows you to play your entire deck in a single turn.
- **Deck cycling:** Calculate your deck's probability. Keep track of how many cards are in your discard pile versus your deck. Advanced players time their purchases to ensure their best cards arrive when they need them for a big buy.
- **Terminal collision:** Be careful with "Terminal Actions" (cards that do not grant +Actions). If you have too many, you will often find yourself with a hand of Action cards you cannot play. Balance your deck composition to keep your action flow smooth.
