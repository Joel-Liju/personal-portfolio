## What I like about it

It is genuienly a good two player cooperative game that people can enjoy. Especially, it is a good game if you like to learn how to communicate without actually using any words. This I argue is the fun part of the game, where you just have to trust that you and your teammates are on the same page, and hope that you don't mess up.

## Notes

One thing I found out is that, for the beginner to medium levels, getting coffee isn't a big deal, but the harder the level is, the less coffee you should probably get, as that would mean that, you can't use them to do other actions. 

Ensure that, you find a way to use the dice without getting coffee, before thinking about getting coffee.
