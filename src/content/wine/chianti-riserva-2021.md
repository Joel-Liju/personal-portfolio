## Tasting Notes & Profile
A complex, medium-to-full-bodied Tuscan red displaying an intense crimson-ruby profile. The bouquet is highly aromatic and spicy, featuring dominant notes of ripe red cherries and dark blackberries underpinned by subtle layers of vanilla, cinnamon, and smoky oak. On the palate, it exhibits a balanced and velvety structure with soft, fine tannins, juicy fruit, and a lingering, mineral-driven finish from its extended aging process.
## Culinary Pairing
Slow-roasted wild boar ragù over fresh pappardelle pasta, or dynamic grilled Bistecca alla Fiorentina.
