## Tasting Notes & Profile
A beautifully structured, medium-bodied Southern Rhône blend that opens with a vibrant, sun-drenched bouquet of ripe blackberries, currants, and garrigue herbs. The palate is juicy and expansive, framed by fine, supple tannins that integrate seamlessly with warm nuances of peppercorns, moka, and traditional baking spices, leading into a fresh, lingering finish.
## Culinary Pairing
Grilled rosemary lamb chops.
