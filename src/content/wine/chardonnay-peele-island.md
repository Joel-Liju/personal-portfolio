## Tasting Notes & Profile
This expressive white displays a pale straw hue with an aromatic profile dominated by intense notes of honeydew melon. On the palate, it delivers a vibrant composition of ripe peach and fresh lime flavours. Medium-bodied with racy acidity, the wine maintains a balanced structure that culminates in a clean, zesty finish.

## Culinary Pairing
Pair with a creamy tuna pasta bake to complement the wine's refreshing acidity and fruit-driven character.
