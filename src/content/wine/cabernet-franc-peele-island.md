## Tasting Notes & Profile
A complex and bold red, this Cabernet Franc offers an enticing nose of tobacco and cedarwood, leading into a palate defined by dark fruits such as cassis and plum. It maintains a medium body with refined tannins and a fresh, structured acidity that creates a well-rounded and persistent finish.

## Culinary Pairing
Pair with charcoal-grilled lamb kebabs to complement the wine's smoky undertones.

## Notes
Notable aroma of tobacco.
