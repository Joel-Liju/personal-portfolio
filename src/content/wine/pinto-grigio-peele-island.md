## Tasting Notes & Profile
This wine offers a refined, vibrant bouquet defined by delicate floral and mineral aromatics. On the palate, it reveals a crisp entry of citrus and orchard fruits—primarily pear and apricot—leading to a silky, balanced texture with a subtle, clean nutty finish.

## Culinary Pairing
Pair with seafood tostada bites or grilled chicken with goat cheese and peppers.
