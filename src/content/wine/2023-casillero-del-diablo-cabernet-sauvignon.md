## Tasting Notes & Profile
This Chilean Cabernet Sauvignon offers a bold and expressive profile, characterized by inviting aromas of ripe blackcurrant, dark cherry, and subtle hints of vanilla and toasted oak. On the palate, it presents a medium-to-full body with firm, well-integrated tannins, delivering flavors of dark plum and chocolate with a lingering, smooth finish.
## Culinary Pairing
Grilled lamb chops or beef tenderloin.
