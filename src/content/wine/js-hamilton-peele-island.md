## Tasting Notes & Profile
A substantial and expressive red blend, the J.S. Hamilton Red presents an intense ruby colour. The palate is defined by a core of dark plum, fig, and blackberry notes, structured by refined tannins and a balanced, lingering finish.

## Culinary Pairing
Pair with fattier cuts of meat like a ribeye steak or slow-cooked pork belly, as well as rich, pungent cheeses such as Stilton or Gorgonzola.
