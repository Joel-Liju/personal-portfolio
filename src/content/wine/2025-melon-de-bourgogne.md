## Tasting Notes & Profile
A wonderfully distinct, cool-climate white wine that showcases the unique limestone soils of Prince Edward County. The nose leads with clean aromas of lemon zest, unripe stone fruits, and a delicate touch of fresh mint. The palate is razor-sharp and mineral-driven, offering crisp flavors of green pear and nectarine supported by a vibrant, saline-tinged acidity and a lean, refreshing finish.
