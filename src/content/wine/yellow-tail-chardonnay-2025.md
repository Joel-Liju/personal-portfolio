## Tasting Notes & Profile
A vibrant, approachable, and fruit-forward white wine displaying generous aromas of ripe peach and melon layered with subtle undertones of vanilla and toasted oak. The palate delivers a smooth, creamy mouthfeel balanced by a soft, zesty finish with a hint of white spice.

## Culinary Pairing
Pairs excellently with roasted chicken breast or a creamy pasta dish.
