## Tasting Notes & Profile
A low-alcohol, approachable expression crafted using spinning cone technology to gently reduce the alcohol content to 8% ABV while retaining classic varietal character. The profile opens with lifted aromas of black cherry and toasty oak, moving into a plush, sweet-toned palate defined by juicy strawberry, dark berries, and a distinct hint of toasted mocha. The texture is smooth and velvety, finishing with silky, well-integrated French oak influences and low acidity.
## Culinary Pairing
Pairs elegantly with seared duck breast drizzled with a sweet cherry reduction.
## Notes
Very light compared to other red wines.
