## Tasting Notes & Profile
A bright and playful estate-grown rosé featuring a vibrant bouquet of ripe red berries, juicy cherry, and raspberry, lifted by delicate wildflower aromas and a subtle whisper of citrus. The palate is soft and juicy with a refreshing, zippy seam of acidity that finishes exceptionally clean.

## Culinary Pairing
Pairs beautifully with a fresh watermelon and feta salad or a classic charcuterie board.
