## Tasting Notes & Profile
A smooth and crowd-pleasing South African red featuring a deep ruby profile. The bouquet opens with fragrant violet and lavender floral dimensions, closely followed by rich aromas of ripe plums, blackberries, and a touch of earth and spice. On the palate, it is medium-bodied with soft, accessible tannins, layering luscious dark berry flavors with trailing undertones of sweet vanilla and melted dark chocolate. 
## Culinary Pairing
Slow-simmered, spice-infused lamb potjiekos or a hearty grilled pepper steak.
