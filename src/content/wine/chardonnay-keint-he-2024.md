## Tasting Notes & Profile
A bright and expressive cool-climate Ontario Chardonnay that delivers a classic, mineral-driven profile. The nose opens with aromas of baked lemon, green apple, and subtle hints of toasty vanilla. On the palate, it balances a clean, crisp texture with a refreshing acidity, offering layers of tree fruit and an elegant, lingering finish with flinty overtones.
## Culinary Pairing
Pan-seared salmon with a lemon-butter glaze.
