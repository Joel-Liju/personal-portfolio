## Tasting Notes & Profile
A medium-bodied Niagara Peninsula red blend showcasing a deep ruby color and an aromatic bouquet of dark cherry, plum, smoky oak, and subtle sweet spice. The palate is fruit-forward with soft, drying tannins that provide excellent structure, leading to a lingering finish accented by notes of black tea, currant, and pomegranate.

## Culinary Pairing
Pairs exceptionally well with grilled red meats, lamb kebobs, or a rich pot roast.
